Life Republic is a planned township in West Pune, built sector by sector over more than a decade. Thousands of families already live here. Echoes is the latest address in it, so the schools, retail streets and open spaces are ready before you move in.

  • Walkable sectors

    Tree-lined internal roads, cycle paths and everyday shops within the township.

    Several schools sit in or next to the township, so the school run stays short.

Where exactly is Echoes?

Inside the Life Republic township at Marunji, on the western edge of Hinjawadi in Pune (PIN 411057).

What homes are available?

2 BHK (813–900 sq ft), 2.5 BHK (963–978 sq ft) and 3 BHK (1,116–1,231 sq ft) carpet area.

When is possession?

The RERA possession date is December 2028. Always confirm the current date on the MahaRERA website.

Is the project RERA registered?

Yes. MahaRERA project no. P52100077008; Echoes Phase 1: PM1261012502409. Look these up at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in.

Are the prices all-inclusive?

No. Listed prices are starting prices. Stamp duty, registration, GST, parking and other charges are extra. Request a cost sheet for exact numbers.
